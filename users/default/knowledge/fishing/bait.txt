Selecting fishing bait

For freshwater float fishing, worms are a useful starting bait for many small fish. Select bait according to the target species and what it naturally eats. Use a small piece of worm on a suitably sized hook; keep the hook point exposed. No bait guarantees a catch.

Fishing bait can also include insects or prepared dough where local rules permit them. Artificial lures imitate prey and require an appropriate retrieval technique. Check restrictions on live bait and never release unused live bait into another water body.
