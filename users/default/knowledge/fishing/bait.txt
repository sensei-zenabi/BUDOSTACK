Fishing bait

Bait is food put on a hook to attract a fish. You can use worms as bait for many small freshwater fish. Choose bait that the fish naturally eat. No bait works for every fish.

Procedure: Step 1 when choosing fishing bait: ask which fish live in the water and what they eat. Worms are a useful starting choice for many small freshwater fish. Check the local rules before using live bait.

Insects and prepared dough can also be fishing bait where the rules allow them. An artificial lure imitates food rather than being food. Never release unused live bait into another lake or river.
