Fishing equipment

Fishing equipment includes a rod, a reel, line, a small hook and bait. A float helps you see a bite. A landing net helps you lift a fish from the water.

Procedure: Step 1 when preparing fishing equipment: ask an adult to help check the rod, reel and line. Choose a hook and line suited to the fish you want to catch. Bring pliers to remove hooks safely.

A fishing rod is the long pole you hold. The reel holds the line. A hook holds the bait and can catch a fish. Hooks are sharp, so ask an adult for help when handling them.
