Basic fishing equipment

For simple float fishing, equipment includes a rod, a reel with suitable line, a small hook, a float, and split-shot weights. Match the line and hook size to the fish you intend to catch. A landing net helps support a fish when lifting it from the water.

Fishing equipment also includes pliers for removing hooks, scissors for cutting line, and a container for discarded line. Wear a personal flotation device near deep or fast water. Check local fishing permissions and rules before choosing a location or method.
