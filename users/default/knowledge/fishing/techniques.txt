Basic fishing

Procedure: To catch a fish:
Step 1: ask an adult to help choose a safe fishing place and check the local rules. Set up your rod, line, float and hook.
Step 2: put bait on the hook and cast gently into clear water. Watch the float for a bite.
Step 3: lift the rod gently and reel the fish in. Keep hooks away from people and handle the fish carefully.

A fishing float sits on the water and helps show when a fish takes the bait. The float is attached to the line. It is different from the rod, which you hold in your hands.

After fishing, take your rubbish and old line home. Line left in the water can harm animals. Release fish promptly unless local rules allow you to keep them.
