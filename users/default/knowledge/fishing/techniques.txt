Basic float fishing procedure

Step 1 for float fishing: choose a safe, permitted fishing spot. Assemble a rod, reel, line, float, weights, and hook. Inspect knots and line for damage. Adjust the float so the bait hangs at the intended depth without dragging along the bottom.

Step 2 for fishing: put bait on the hook, check that the casting area is clear, and cast gently. Allow the float to settle. Keep slack line under control and watch for a sustained movement indicating a bite.

Step 3 for fishing: lift the rod to set the hook gently and reel steadily while maintaining line tension. Use a landing net if needed. Handle fish with wet hands and minimize time out of water. Release fish promptly or retain only fish allowed by local rules.
